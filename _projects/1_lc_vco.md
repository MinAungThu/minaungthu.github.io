A complementary LC voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) designed to cover the 5.226–5.855 GHz band, implemented in TSMC 0.18 µm CMOS technology, with a 3-bit switched-capacitor bank and low K_VCO variation. Conducted under the supervision of <a href="https://ee.ccu.edu.tw/p/412-1097-667.php?Lang=en" target="_blank">Professor Janne-Wha Wu</a> at the <a href="https://sites.google.com/a/ee.ccu.edu.tw/wireless-communication-lab/home?authuser=0" target="_blank">Wireless Communication Lab</a>, National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan.
